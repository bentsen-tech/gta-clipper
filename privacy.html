1. Who we are

GTA Clipper by Bentsen ("the App", "we", "us") is a desktop software tool operated by Tobias Bentsen, an independent developer based in Denmark. Tobias Bentsen is the data controller for personal data processed through the App and this website. Contact: bentsen@nextlevel-sales.dk.

2. Scope

This policy explains how we collect, use, store, share and delete information when you (a) connect a TikTok or Google/YouTube account to the App, (b) appear in content processed by the App, or (c) visit this website. The App is used by its operator to post short GTA 6 highlight videos to the operator's own social media accounts.

3. Information we process

3.1 TikTok account information

When you connect a TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, we request only the scopes user.info.basic and video.publish. We receive and store:

DataSourcePurpose
Access token, refresh token, token expiryTikTok OAuthTo post on your behalf after you confirm a post
Open ID, display name, username, avatar URLTikTok user infoTo show which account is connected
Creator info: available privacy levels, comment/Duet/Stitch settings, maximum video lengthTikTok creator info endpointTo show the correct options on the posting screen
Publish ID, post status, post ID/link, caption and the privacy setting you selectedTikTok Content Posting APITo confirm the post and show its status

We do not access your followers, messages, likes, watch history, contacts or any other TikTok data.

3.2 Google/YouTube account information

If you connect YouTube, we request only the youtube.upload scope and store the OAuth tokens and the ID, title, description, tags and status of videos uploaded by the App.

3.3 Public Twitch content

The App processes publicly available Twitch data for the streamers the operator chooses: stream status and viewer counts, public clips, and public chat messages. Chat messages are analysed on the operator's computer only to calculate aggregate activity statistics (for example messages per second and number of active chatters). Individual chat messages and chatter usernames are not stored. Public clips may contain the voice and image of streamers; see section 13.

3.4 Website visitors

This website is a static site hosted by GitHub Pages. We do not use cookies, analytics or tracking. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to operate and secure its service; see GitHub's privacy statement.

3.5 Communications

If you email us, we process your email address and the content of your message to respond.

4. How we use information

  • To connect your TikTok or YouTube account and show which account is connected.
  • To show the posting options TikTok makes available for your account, and to post a video only after you review it and click Post.
  • To check and display the status of a post and avoid posting the same video twice.
  • To detect highlight moments from aggregate Twitch chat activity.
  • To respond to questions, removal requests and privacy requests.

We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising or profiling, or use TikTok data for any purpose other than those listed above.

5. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Performance of a contract / your request (Art. 6(1)(b)): processing your TikTok/YouTube data to post content you ask us to post.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): the authorization you give in the TikTok or Google consent screen. You can withdraw it at any time (section 11).
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): processing public Twitch data in aggregate to find highlights and crediting streamers, and keeping our systems secure.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): where we must keep or disclose information by law.

6. Sharing and service providers

We share data only as needed to provide the App:

  • TikTok – videos, captions and post settings are sent to TikTok when you click Post.
  • Google/YouTube – videos and metadata are sent when uploading to YouTube.
  • Dropbox and Metricool – may be used by the operator to temporarily host finished videos and schedule posts. They receive the video file and caption, not your TikTok tokens.
  • Authorities – if required by law.

TikTok tokens and TikTok user data are never shared with any third party.

7. International transfers

TikTok, Google, Dropbox and GitHub may process data outside the EU/EEA. Where they do, they rely on safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

9. Security

Data is stored locally on a password-protected computer. Tokens are kept in local files that are excluded from source control and never shared. Connections to TikTok, Google and other providers use HTTPS.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, email bentsen@nextlevel-sales.dk. We respond within 30 days. You may also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet, www.datatilsynet.dk) or the authority in your country.

11. Disconnecting TikTok or Google

You can revoke the App's access at any time:

  • TikTok: TikTok app → Profile → ☰ → Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions → remove the App.
  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions.

After revocation, the related tokens and account data are deleted within 7 days. You can also email us to request deletion.

12. Children

The App and this website are not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly process children's personal data. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Streamers and content removal

Videos made with the App are based on public Twitch clips and always credit the streamer. If you are a streamer (or appear in a clip) and want a video removed or your data deleted, email bentsen@nextlevel-sales.dk with a link. We remove it within 48 hours.

14. Cookies

This website and the App do not use cookies, tracking pixels or analytics.

15. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version and its effective date are always shown on this page. Significant changes are highlighted on the homepage.
