GTA Clipper

A private, internal tool that turns the best moments from GTA 6 Twitch streams into vertical videos and posts them to its operator's own TikTok account and YouTube channel.

Uses TikTok Login Kit & Content Posting API

Uses YouTube API Services (video upload only)

How it works

  1. Watches selected Twitch streams and detects highlight moments from chat activity.
  2. Downloads the official Twitch clip and edits it into a 1080×1920 video with subtitles and streamer credit.
  3. The operator reviews the video, chooses the privacy settings, and the tool posts it to the operator's own TikTok account (Content Posting API) and YouTube channel (YouTube Data API).

GTA Clipper is not available to the public. It runs only on the operator's own computer.
