Turn the best GTA 6 stream moments into ready-to-post vertical videos.

GTA Clipper by Bentsen is a desktop tool that finds highlight moments in GTA 6 Twitch streams, edits them into 9:16 videos with subtitles and streamer credit, and lets the creator review and post them to their own TikTok account.

Features

Everything runs on the creator's own computer. The tool handles the repetitive editing work, so the creator can focus on choosing the best moments.

Highlight detection

Watches the creator's chosen streamers and detects peaks in chat activity and viewer numbers that signal a highlight moment.

Official Twitch clips

Uses Twitch's own clip feature to capture the moment, so the source is always traceable to the original stream.

Vertical editing

Automatically formats each clip as a 1080×1920 video with the streamer's facecam on top and the gameplay below.

Readable subtitles

Generates subtitles locally with speech recognition and filters offensive words before export.

Copyright checks

Detects background music and flags clips that may contain copyrighted songs, before anything is posted.

Review & post

The creator reviews each video in a local dashboard, chooses who can see it and confirms before it is posted to TikTok.

How it works

Posting to TikTok always requires the creator's explicit action and consent.

  1. Choose streamers. The creator adds the Twitch channels they want to follow in the dashboard.
  2. Clips are found and edited. When a highlight is detected, the official Twitch clip is downloaded and edited into a vertical video with subtitles and a credit line (twitch.tv/streamer).
  3. Connect TikTok. The creator signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorizes the app. The dashboard then shows the connected account's name and avatar.
  4. Review the post. For each video, the posting screen shows the video preview and caption, and lets the creator choose the privacy level (nothing is pre-selected), turn comments, Duet and Stitch on or off, and disclose commercial content.
  5. Post. Only when the creator clicks Post is the video uploaded with TikTok's Content Posting API. The dashboard then shows the post status.

Responsible use

Credit every streamer

Every caption names the streamer and links to their Twitch channel. Streamers can ask for any video to be removed at any time.

Own accounts only

The tool only posts to TikTok accounts the signed-in creator owns. It never posts without the creator's confirmation.

Minimal data

Only the scopes needed to post are requested. Tokens and data stay on the creator's own computer. No ads, no tracking.

Frequently asked questions

Who is GTA Clipper by Bentsen for?

It is a personal tool used by its operator to run a GTA 6 highlights account. It is not sold or offered as a public service.

Does it post to TikTok automatically without my approval?

No. Each post is reviewed in the posting screen, where the creator selects the privacy level and interaction settings and confirms by clicking Post.

What TikTok data does it use?

Only the connected account's basic profile (display name, avatar, open ID) and the creator information needed to show the posting options. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I disconnect TikTok?

In the TikTok app go to Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions, and remove the app. Stored tokens are deleted within 7 days.

I am a streamer and want a video removed.

Email bentsen@nextlevel-sales.dk with a link to the video. We remove it within 48 hours.
